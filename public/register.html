By registering for the GCIT Innovation Week Web Design Workshop you agree to the following.

  1. Eligibility. Registration is open to GCIT students and staff with a valid GCIT email address.
  2. Registration. Registration is for the Web Design Workshop. The details you give must be accurate. Organisers may remove duplicate or incorrect registrations.
  3. Photo, video and audio consent. You allow the organisers to take photos, videos and audio recordings at the workshop and to use them in GCIT reports, on the college website and social media, and in promotional material.   9. Changes. The organisers may change or cancel the workshop and will inform registered participants using the contact details you provided.
